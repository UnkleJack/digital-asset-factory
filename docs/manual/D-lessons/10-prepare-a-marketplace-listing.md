# Lesson 10: Prepare a marketplace listing

**Verification:** listings were drafted and read live; the editor was captured (VERIFIED). Editing and saving a listing was NOT clicked. Fee numbers were read from the marketplaces' pages on 2026-11-17; only you can confirm they still match.

## Learning objective
Review a drafted listing, understand the marketplace's limits, and read the "what you keep" line correctly.

## What we are creating
The Etsy listing for the sample mandala pack.

## What we need
A packaged product (any Ready product has listing drafts).

## Steps
1. Open **Marketplace listings**. Filter **Etsy**. Click the mandala row.
2. **Title:** read it against the counter. (Live: a 126-character title inside Etsy's 140.)
3. **Tags:** Etsy allows at most 13 tags of 20 characters. **A longer tag is dropped** and noted ("Dropped tag longer than 20 characters"). Shorten the tags you care about.
4. **Description:** at least 150 characters. Read it as a buyer; check for claims you cannot support.
5. **Price:** read **What you keep after the marketplace's fees**.
6. **Save changes** (only enabled after you edit).
7. If the listing was already approved, **saving withdraws the approval**. Approve again in Batch approval.

## Expected results
Status **Ready**, counters within limits, no red messages.

## What to inspect
- The fee line leaves out advertising, tax, refunds, currency conversion and your cost of making the product. It is **not profit**.
- A $5.99 Etsy listing showed **$5.17 per sale (86%) plus $0.20 to list it** (live).
- A non-USD price shows "not available".

## Common mistakes
Editing after approval and forgetting; ticking Gumroad and Generic without using them; trusting AI-written claims. The Constitution forbids invented sales or review claims in the marketing kit; do the same in listing copy.

## Completion criteria
- [ ] Title, tags and description within limits.
- [ ] I know what the fee line excludes.
- [ ] Approval re-done if I edited an approved listing.

## What I learned
Listings are drafts the app checks against marketplace rules; editing them after approval resets the approval.
