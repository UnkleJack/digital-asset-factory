# Lesson 8: Rework an existing product

**Verification:** Regenerate with a blank note and the Rework list were exercised live (VERIFIED). **Return for revision**, **Reject** and **Edit content** need an AI key or were not clicked, so they are marked below (NOT VERIFIED).

## Learning objective
Use the three ways to change a finished product, know which cancel an approval, and know what notes teach the AI.

## What we are creating
A second version of a habit tracker. Practice brief 8, *Semester Momentum Habit Tracker* ([practice briefs](00-the-twelve-practice-briefs.md)), is a good subject; it does not exist yet.

## What we need
A finished product waiting in Batch approval. For revision and reject notes, a **working AI key** (it checks notes are specific).

## The three routes

| Route | Where | What it does | Needs AI? | Approval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Regenerate** | Batch approval | New version, **new random artwork**, same settings | No (blank note works, verified) | The old version is superseded |
| **Return for revision** | Batch approval | New version built using your notes | Yes (note is AI-checked) | Old version superseded |
| **Edit content** / **Change design / paper** | Product page | New version with edited content or look | No | **Cancels any approval of the current version** |

## Steps
1. Open the product's batch, **Review**, press **Regenerate** with a blank note.
2. A new version **v2** is made; **v1 shows as superseded**.
3. Open **Rework**. Under **Rejected by you** the older version appears as "v1 of 2 (older version), Regenerated".
4. Press **Add note** and write what you learned ("Rows too small to tick with a pen"). **Save note**.
5. Press **Mark fixed**.
6. Review v2 in Batch approval and approve it.

**With an AI key:** use **Return for revision** with a specific note ("The month header overlaps the first row. Make the header smaller."). Draw a box on the page to show *which part*.

## Expected results (live)
After Regenerate: the product listed **v2 Ready / QA Passed** and v1 *superseded*; Rework counted the older version.

## What to inspect
v2 against v1: did it improve the thing you noted? Regenerate changes artwork or wording randomly, not deliberately.

## Common mistakes
Regenerating to fix a *setting* problem (see [Lesson 7](07-run-qa-and-fix-a-problem.md)); writing "bad" as a note; editing an approved product without knowing it cancels the approval.

## Completion criteria
- [ ] I made v2 and approved it.
- [ ] I added a note and marked the old item fixed.
- [ ] I know which routes cancel approvals.

## What I learned
Every change creates a **new version**; the old one is kept. Notes are the only way the AI learns your taste.
