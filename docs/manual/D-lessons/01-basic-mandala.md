# Lesson 1: Create a basic mandala coloring product

**Verification:** the product, its files and QA were produced and read in a throwaway copy (VERIFIED live). The full click-by-click is in [Your first product](../A-start-here/03-your-first-product.md); this lesson adds the practice and the "why".

## Learning objective
Make one procedurally drawn product, read its QA, and learn which setting changes which part of the result.

## What we are creating
A six-design celestial mandala pack. This is the first of your twelve practice briefs ([Celestial Geometry Mandala Collection](00-the-twelve-practice-briefs.md)); it does not exist yet.

## What we need before starting
The app running. No AI key and no cost.

## Steps
1. Product library → **New product** → **Coloring Pages** → **Mandala coloring pages**.
2. Title: `Sample Celestial Mandala Collection`. Pages 6, Detail medium, Symmetry auto, **Motif set celestial**, Cover on.
3. Look at the **SAMPLE** panel. Press **Different designs** twice; Create keeps the seed you see.
4. Tick Etsy. Press **Create and start production**.
5. Wait for **v1 Ready** and **QA Passed**.
6. Change one thing and compare: **Make variation** with Detail **detailed**. (A variation always gets new random art, so judge it by detail level, not by exact shapes.)

## Expected results
- Six succeeded steps; a **7-page PDF** (cover plus 6), six SVGs, a ZIP, listings. (Live: PDF 164 KB, SVGs 7 to 18 KB.)
- QA about 20 checks, all passed. Thinnest printed line about **0.46 mm** against a 0.2 mm minimum.

## What to inspect
Open each page of the PDF. Count how many areas you could not color with a pencil. Compare the medium and detailed pages.

## Common mistakes
Expecting the Celestial *theme* to change drawings; judging only from the small previews; ticking Gumroad and Generic you will not use.

## Completion criteria
- [ ] I made the product and it passed QA.
- [ ] I can say what Detail, Symmetry and Motif set change.
- [ ] I made a variation and compared the two.

## What I learned
Procedural artwork is repeatable and free; **QA proves the lines are printable, not that the page is pretty**; a variation changes the seed.
