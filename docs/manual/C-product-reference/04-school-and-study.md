# School and study

Three types added 2026-11-10. The AI writes their words when a key works; otherwise the built-in library does, so they work offline. **Times, numbering and page fit are always computed by the app.**

**Verification:** all three were made in the throwaway copy and passed QA; form fields read live (VERIFIED). Not yet printed on paper (the Constitution says so).

---

## Assignment tracker (`assignment-tracker`)

**1.** A "My subjects" page with tips, then log pages (subject, assignment, set, due, done, grade). Students. Price 3.49. Design Soft Pastel.

**2. Settings** (form fields confirmed live: Layout, Pages, Rows per page, Show grade, Cover)

| Setting | Controls | Range, default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Layout | **combined** (one running list) or **per-subject** (a page each) | combined |
| Pages | Log pages (combined only) | 1 to 20, **4** |
| Rows per page | Rows (reduced automatically if too small to write in, with a QA warning) | 8 to 30, **18** |
| Show grade | Grade column | on |
| Subjects | Your own subject list (3 to 12) | **not on the form** |
| Cover | Title page | on |

**3.** AI writes the cover subtitle, intro, subject list and 3 to 6 tips; offline from the school or kids library by audience. **4. Files.** PDF (cover, subjects page, logs).

**5. Inspect.** QA: 3 to 12 subjects and 3 to 6 tips (hard); duplicate subjects, subjects you listed not matching those printed, and rows too small (warnings). **Check the rows are big enough to write in once printed.** **6.** Preset *Student assignment tracker (page per subject)*. **7. Example.** Defaults, audience Students. ("The Assignment Launchpad Checklist" fits this type or To-do: choose one.)

## Class timetable (`timetable`)

**1.** A weekly grid with period times, a subjects key and a notes box. Price 2.99. Design Bold Modern.

**2. Settings**

| Setting | Controls | Range, default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days | Columns | 5, 6 or 7, **5** |
| Week start | Sunday or Monday | **Monday** |
| Periods | Rows | 3 to 12, **6** |
| Start time | 24-hour HH:MM | **08:30** |
| Period minutes | Length | 20 to 120, **50** |
| Break minutes | Gap between periods | 0 to 30, **10** |
| Lunch after | Which period lunch follows (0 = none) | 0 to 11, **3** |
| Lunch minutes | | 15 to 90, **40** |
| Weeks | **single** or **ab** (Week A and Week B pages) | single |
| Show times | Print period times | on |

All times are worked out from these. A lunch set after the last period is not printed.

**3.** AI writes the subtitle, 4 to 12 subjects and a notes label; offline from the library. **4. Files.** PDF of 1 page (2 for A/B), plus a page if the subjects key does not fit.

**5. Inspect.** QA (all hard unless noted): times in order and the right length, days right, grid fits, subject key 4 to 12; lunch where asked (warning). **Compare the times with your real school day.** **6.** If the key will not fit: bigger paper, fewer periods or no times. Presets: *Class timetable (Mon-Fri, 6 periods)*, *Two-week (A/B) class timetable*. **7. Example.** Defaults (live: passed).

## Reading log (`reading-log`)

**1.** A numbered book list, reading challenge, monthly reading-minutes pages and book-review pages. Price 3.49. Design Warm Neutral.

**2. Settings** (form fields confirmed live: Books, Challenge, Minutes months, Review pages, Cover)

| Setting | Controls | Range, default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Books | Numbered rows | 10 to 100, **30** |
| Goal | Prints a reading goal | optional, **not on the form** |
| Challenge | One book of each kind | on |
| Minutes months | Undated minutes pages | 0 to 12, **3** |
| Review pages | | 0 to 20, **4** |
| Cover | | on |

**3.** AI writes the cover subtitle, 6 to 16 challenge genres and 3 to 6 review prompts; offline from the library (general or kids). **4. Files.** PDF.

**5. Inspect.** QA: books numbered 1 to your count with none missing, genre and prompt counts (hard); goal fits inside the book count (warning). Check the challenge genres suit the age. **6.** Presets *Student reading log* (sets a goal), *Kids reading log*. **7. Example.** Books 30, challenge on, minutes months 3, review pages 4 (live: passed).
