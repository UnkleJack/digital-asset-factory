# Activities and coloring

Six types. Four are drawn by algorithms or your own pictures; only Illustrated coloring needs an image AI.

**Verification:** mandala, maze, word search and geometric were made in the throwaway copy and their files and QA read (VERIFIED live) except the geometric/word-search page counts, which come from the code. Illustrated coloring was NOT run (it would call an outside image service). Imported coloring was run with three sample pictures (VERIFIED live).

---

## Mandala coloring pages (`mandala-coloring`)

**1. What it makes.** A coloring book of mandalas (circular designs with mirror symmetry). For adult relaxation coloring and teachers. Price 3.99. Default design Minimal Mono.

**2. Settings**

| Setting | Controls | Range, default | Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pages | Number of designs | 1 to 30, **10** | No |
| Detail | Rings: simple 3, medium 5, detailed 7, intricate 10 | **medium** | No |
| Symmetry | Repeats around the centre: auto, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 18, 20, 24 | **auto** | No |
| Motif set | Shape family: classic, botanical, geometric, celestial (14 motifs in total) | **classic** | No |
| Cover | Title page | on | No |

(An older `complexity` setting is still read as Detail.) **What changes:** more Detail means finer lines and more to color; Symmetry changes the number of petals; Motif set changes the family of shapes. Theme does **not** change the drawings.

**3. AI or algorithm.** **No AI.** Titles ("Lotus Bloom" style) come from a built-in list. Each page is `procedural-mandala@2`, a 1000 x 1000 SVG drawn from a seed (seed + page x 7919). Designs are true mirror images.

**4. Files.** PDF (a cover plus one page per design: **6 pages gives 7 PDF pages**), one SVG per design, instructions, license, ZIP. *Live:* 6 SVGs of 7 to 17 KB.

**5. Inspect.** **Open every page.** Look for areas too fiddly to color and designs that feel the same. QA checks lines stay inside the box (hard), no line thinner than 0.2 mm printed (hard; live thinnest 0.46 mm), black on white only (hard) and density (warning: over 20,000 path segments or rings under 2 mm).

**6. Problems and improvements.** Too busy: lower Detail. Pages look alike: use **Make variation** (new seed) or **Different designs** in the sample panel. Print limits are starting values not yet checked against real prints; print a page.

**7. Example.** Pages 6, Detail medium, Symmetry auto, Motif set celestial. (Used in [Your first product](../A-start-here/03-your-first-product.md).)

## Maze activity book (`maze-activity`)

**1.** A book of one-solution mazes with an answer key. For kids and teachers. Price 3.49. Design Kids Bright.

**2. Settings**

| Setting | Controls | Range, default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pages | Number of mazes | 1 to 30, **10** |
| Difficulty | Grid size: easy 10 x 13, medium 16 x 21, hard 24 x 32 cells | **medium** |
| Solutions | Add answer pages | **on** |

**3. AI or algorithm.** **No AI.** `procedural-maze@1` makes perfect mazes (exactly one route). Titles are "Maze 1, Maze 2...". Theme has no effect: **type the title yourself** ("Mission: Space Rescue Maze Pack").

**4. Files.** PDF only for the customer: mazes plus ceil(pages / 4) answer pages (*live: 4 mazes gave a 5-page PDF*). The SVGs are **internal and not in the ZIP**.

**5. Inspect.** QA proves each maze is perfect and the counts are right (hard). You check the answer pages match the right maze and that the difficulty suits the age.

**6.** Hard grids may be too small a path width for young children; use easy. 

**7. Example.** Pages 4, Difficulty easy, title typed by you.

## Word search puzzles (`word-search`)

**1.** Themed word-search puzzles with answer pages. Kids and classroom. Price 3.49.

**2. Settings**

| Setting | Controls | Range, default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Puzzles | How many | 1 to 30, **8** |
| Grid size | Squares per side | 8 to 20, **14** |
| Words per puzzle | | 5 to 16, **12** |
| Difficulty | Directions: easy = right and down; medium adds diagonals; hard adds reversed directions | **medium** |
| Solutions | Answer pages | **on** |
| Topic | Word list topic | optional, **not on the form** (use **Theme**) |

**3. AI or algorithm.** The **words** come from the AI (A to Z only, 3 to 12 letters) or, offline, from 10 built-in lists of 16 words: ocean, space, dinosaurs, autumn, winter, spring, animals, kitchen, sports, weather. The list is chosen by matching your **Theme** name (or topic). A theme with no list (for example Celestial) falls back to **animals**. More than 10 puzzles repeat lists. The **grid** is always generated by the app.

**4. Files.** PDF only: puzzles plus ceil(puzzles / 2) answer pages.

**5. Inspect.** **Read every word list.** QA proves each word is in the grid and none is too long (hard). It cannot tell whether a word is too hard or odd for the topic.

**6.** With **Theme: Ocean**, offline words come from the ocean list (verified the theme exists; the list match is from the code). Offline, keep puzzles at 10 or fewer to avoid repeats.

**7. Example.** Theme Ocean, puzzles 4, grid 14, words 12, difficulty medium. ("Ocean Explorer Word Search".)

## Illustrated coloring pages (`illustrated-coloring`)

**1.** AI-drawn black-and-white line art scenes (woodland, ocean, space, dinosaurs, botanical). For kids and adults. Price 4.49.

**2. Settings**

| Setting | Controls | Range, default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pages | Pictures | 1 to 20, **6** |
| Fit | **sharp** caps print size at 200 DPI; **fill** fills the page and QA checks DPI | **sharp** |
| Cover | Title page | on |

**3. AI or algorithm.** **AI-drawn pictures** (needs an image provider). Scene descriptions come from the text AI or, offline, **6 fixed scenes per theme**. An early-reader audience asks for thick lines.

**4. Files.** PDF plus one PNG per page in the ZIP. Every call is recorded with provenance and estimated cost.

**5. Inspect.** **Every picture.** QA never sees what is drawn. Look for cropped faces and fins, odd expressions, stray text, the wrong animal. QA warns on gray tones above 15 percent, any color above 5 percent, and low DPI.

**6. Problems.** **More than 6 pages offline gives only 6 scenes, so QA's picture count fails (worked out from the code; NOT VERIFIED live).** The Dashboard's Image AI row shows which provider would answer. The default first choice (OpenAI) is paid; the backup chain includes keyless Pollinations and the paid ones are skipped unless **allowPaidFallback** is on. Whether Pollinations actually produces usable line art is not proven.

**7. Example (practice).** Theme Ocean, pages 6, fit sharp, audience Kids 4-8. Run [Lesson 4](../D-lessons/04-illustrated-coloring-pages.md) only after you have tested an image provider.

## Geometric pattern art (`geometric-pattern-art`)

**1.** Pattern pages, either **coloring** line art or **print** wall art. Coloring books and decor printables. Price 3.99.

**2. Settings**

| Setting | Controls | Range, default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pages | Number of pages | 1 to 30, **8** |
| Family | tessellate, girih, opart, truchet, guilloche, fractal, lace, herringbone (2 to 4 variants each; pages cycle through them) | **tessellate** |
| Mode | **coloring**: black lines with a page title. **print**: filled with the design system's colors, the picture alone on the page | **coloring** |
| Density | light, medium, dense | **medium** |
| Invert | Light lines on a dark ground (print only) | off |
| Cover | Title page | on |

**3. AI or algorithm.** **No AI.** `procedural-pattern@1` draws each page from a seed (seed + page x 6151). Titles come from word pools.

**4. Files.** PDF plus one SVG per page. Listing copy changes with the mode.

**5. Inspect.** Each pattern is a complete drawing inside its box, lines are thick enough, coloring pages are black on white (all hard); print pages need 3:1 contrast (hard); density is a warning. Judge the look on screen and, for print mode, the colors on your printer.

**6.** Presets: **Geometric coloring book** and **Geometric wall art prints**.

**7. Example.** Family girih, mode coloring, density medium, pages 4. (Live: this setup made a product that passed QA.)

## Coloring pages from your imports (`imported-coloring`)

**1.** A coloring product built from pictures you made elsewhere. **Not on the New product form**: made from **Imports** ([Lesson 3](../D-lessons/03-import-outside-artwork.md)). Price: no saved price, a built-in fallback of 4.99 applies.

**2. Settings**

| Setting | Controls | Default | Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| References | The picture ids, in page order (up to 40) | none | **Yes** |
| Fit | **fill** (default here, unlike illustrated) or sharp | fill | No |
| Cover | Title page | on | No |

**3. AI or algorithm.** **No AI.** The subtitle is "N designs to color". The AI-disclosure line on listings counts a picture as AI only if you stored a prompt with it.

**4. Files.** PDF plus one PNG per picture in the ZIP. *Live: 3 pictures gave a 4-page PDF.*

**5. Inspect.** Each picture made a clean page; QA warns on gray tones, color and low print resolution (warnings only).

**6. Problems.** A listing-spelling warning appeared live ("possible misspellings artwork"): the machine's dictionary lacked "artwork". Accept it or add the word to the QA allowlist.

**7. Example.** Three tagged `mandala` pictures, title "Sample Imported Art Coloring Pages".
