# Bundle (`bundle`)

**Verification:** a four-product pack was built and passed QA in the throwaway copy; its ZIP was read (VERIFIED live). AI-written bundle copy needs a key and was NOT run.

## 1. What it makes
A **pack**: several approved products sold together as one download. Price 12.99. Design Minimal Mono. Packs are made by **Build a pack** on the Product library or by a **cart** on the Factory floor, **not** from the New product form.

## 2. Settings

| Setting | Controls | Range | Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Members | The products inside | **2 to 20** | **Yes** |
| Pitch | Optional description of the pack | up to 1200 characters | No |

Rules: every member must be **approved**, QA-passed and have any warnings accepted. **Packs cannot nest.** A product can be in at most 4 packs and waits 30 days before joining another with mostly the same tags. A bundle cannot have variations.

## 3. AI or algorithm
AI writes a cover subtitle and one blurb per member (and can suggest the pack title and description, needing a key). Offline: a generic "N printables in one download" subtitle and blurbs built from the member titles and page counts.

## 4. Files
A **2-page "What's inside" contents guide PDF** (not a combined PDF), and a ZIP holding that guide plus **each member's own deliverables in its own folder**. *Live, four members:* `sample-student-starter-pack.pdf`, then folders `sample-weekly-planner/`, `sample-class-timetable/`, `sample-goal-planner/` and `sample-mandala-coloring-pages/` (with its six SVGs), plus LICENSE, HOW-TO-USE and MANIFEST.

## 5. Inspect before approving
QA: one blurb per member (hard) and every member passed QA and has its files (hard). You check the members **belong together** and the price makes sense. Open the guide.

## 6. Common problems
- "Not enough approved products": approve at least two.
- A member you edited after approving it has lost its approval (any material edit cancels an approval), so it no longer qualifies. Re-approve it first.
- A pack of unrelated items sells poorly; choose a theme or angle.
